01 / How it works

Four steps. One pool.

Step two runs today as a regular non-custodial swap. The shielded steps ship after independent audits.

Shield Phase 2

Deposit SOL, USDC or USDT. You receive a private note only you can spend.

02

Swap Live

03

Need another chain? A solver fills it from inventory. You never bridge.

04

Unshield Phase 2

Withdraw to a fresh address with a proof that hides which deposit you spent.

One static site.

Live

Non-custodial

Your wallet signs each swap. DankSwap never holds keys or funds, and there is no account to create.

Live

Minimum-out protection

Every trade carries a slippage limit. If the price moves past it, the transaction fails and nothing moves.
