Unlocking asks your wallet to sign a fixed message. It is free, sends nothing, and the signature never leaves this page. The same wallet always unlocks the same private balance.

    Your shielded address
    —

    Share it to receive private sends. It does not reveal your wallet.

    Move tokens from your wallet into your private balance. Your wallet signs and pays the network fee; the deposit itself is public, what you do next is not.

    Send from your private balance to another shielded address. Nobody else sees the amount or the recipient. A relayer submits it, so your wallet never appears.

    Withdraw from your private balance to any Solana address. A relayer submits it and takes the fee shown, so the destination is not linked to your wallet.

    Use this only if the relayer is unavailable. Your connected wallet signs and pays about 0.002 SOL, and it becomes visible in the withdrawal transaction, linking it to the destination.

    Private swaps route through Jupiter, which only exists on Solana mainnet. They stay off in this devnet preview and on mainnet until the pool is audited.
